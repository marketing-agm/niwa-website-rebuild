    Pricing, screening & legal disclosures

    All pricing, availability, fees, and lease terms are subject to change prior to the execution of a lease agreement. Photographs, renderings, and floor plans are for illustrative purposes and may vary by unit. Information is provided in good faith and is subject to verification.

    For Seattle properties, applications are processed in accordance with Seattle Municipal Code (SMC) 14.08 (First-in-Time Ordinance). For non-Seattle Washington properties, applications are processed in the order received, and all Washington properties comply with RCW 59.18.257 regarding tenant screening disclosures, including notice that comprehensive reusable tenant screening reports are not accepted, as applicable based on the property's jurisdiction and governing municipal regulations.

    Applicants are screened for a credit score of 650 or higher with minimal negative reporting, 1–2 years of positive rental history, and a collective income of 3× the monthly rent. Consider this statement as notification that we do not accept a comprehensive reusable tenant screening report, as defined by RCW 59.18.257.
